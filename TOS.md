The Bot is operated as a private hobby project and not by a registered company.  
**There is no entitlement or guaranteed right to use the Bot.**

## 2. Purpose of the Bot
Tournament Assistant is designed to support tournaments hosted on the OPL platform by automating and simplifying organizational tasks. Features include:

- Setting up an Organizer  
- Changing the Organizer  
- Deleting the Organizer  
- Creating match channels  
- Automatic team roles and nickname adjustments  
- Listing upcoming tournaments  
- Providing an Organizer overview  
- Additional tools for tournament management  

The feature set may be changed, expanded, or removed at any time.

## 7. Subscription Tiers
To cover operational costs, the Bot offers optional subscription tiers.

### 7.1 Automation Tier
Unlocks automated background features such as:

- Automatic match-related notifications  
- Automatic reminders  
- Repeating commands during active matches  
- Additional automation tools  

### 7.2 Point System Tier
Unlocks the advanced penalty point system:

- Add penalty points to players or teams  
- Automatic role assignment at defined thresholds  
- Automatic notifications in a configured channel  
- Automatic point decay over time
